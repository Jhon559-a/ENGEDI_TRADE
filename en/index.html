What happens if the minimum isn't reached?

We let you know 48 hours before the session. You choose: a 100% refund or moving your seat to the next date.

Can I cancel?

Up to 72 hours before the session: full refund. After that there is no refund, but you still receive the summary with photos and prices.

Are the prices shown final?

They are prices quoted by the shops on the day of the session. The final price depends on quantity, contract, packing and shipping.

Is the session recorded?

Only when the shops allow it. Recordings are shared with participants only.

What we don't do
  • We don't source or show counterfeits or products bearing third-party brands.
  • We are not customs brokers or carriers. We can introduce partners for that part.
  • We don't book flights or hotels for you.
